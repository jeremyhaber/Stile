Unused tabs in this profile move to the archive. Tabs pinned to Favourites or Pinned are never archived.

Tabs inside tab groups are kept by default, including when you press Clear.

Closing a pop-out that has a single tab archives that tab instead. The main window is the first window of the session; right-click a window in Today to change it.

After a restart, Favourites and Pinned tabs that had wandered off go back to their original page. The back button (↶) still resets one by hand.
